## ✨ About

**Maximize Magic** is a mobile app for Android and iOS that gives you live wait times for all major Orlando theme parks — Walt Disney World, Universal, SeaWorld, and more.

No account needed. No hassle. Just open the app, check the times, and go enjoy the magic.

Built with **Kotlin Multiplatform**, delivering a native experience on both Android and iOS from a single codebase.

---

## 🎢 Features

- ⏱️ **Live wait times** — real-time data updated continuously
- 🗺️ **All parks in one place** — Disney, Universal, SeaWorld and more
- 📱 **Android & iOS** — native experience on both platforms
- 🔒 **Privacy first** — no account, no tracking, no personal data collected

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Your Data

We believe your trust is as important as your experience inside the parks.

**Maximize Magic is built with a privacy-first philosophy:**

- ✅ No account or registration required
- ✅ No personal data collected or stored
- ✅ No data sold to third parties — ever
- ✅ Location is only used locally and never transmitted
- ✅ Anonymous crash diagnostics only, to improve app stability

We are fully transparent about how the app works and what (very little) data it touches. You can read our full policies below.
