Privacy Policy

Maximize Magic · Orlando Parks

Last updated: May 12, 2026
Welcome to Maximize Magic — your companion for real-time wait times at Orlando theme parks. We believe your privacy is as important as the magic itself. This policy explains clearly what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.
📋

1. Information We Collect

Maximize Magic is designed to work with minimal data. Sign-in is optional — you may use the app as a guest without providing any personal information.

  • Account information (optional): if you choose to sign in with Facebook or Google, we receive your name and email address from those services solely to identify your session within the app. We do not store this information on our own servers.
  • Usage data: anonymous, aggregated information about app interactions (screens viewed, features used) to improve the experience.
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, and app version — used solely for crash diagnostics.
  • Location (optional): only if you explicitly grant permission, to show your nearest park. We never store or transmit your location.
  • Purchase information: if you subscribe to a Premium plan, your purchase is processed by Apple and managed by RevenueCat. We receive only the subscription status (active or inactive) — we never access your payment card details.
🔒

2. How We Use Your Information

Any data collected is used exclusively to:

  • Display accurate, real-time wait times for Orlando theme parks.
  • Identify your session when you sign in with Facebook or Google.
  • Manage your Premium subscription status.
  • Show contextual advertisements to users on ad-supported plans (no personalized tracking is used).
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues or app crashes.
  • Improve app performance and user experience over time.

We do not sell your data to third parties or use it for profiling.

🌐

3. Third-Party Services

Maximize Magic integrates the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • themeparks API: open-source API used to retrieve real-time wait time data. No personal user data is sent to this service.
  • Facebook Login: used for optional sign-in. When you log in with Facebook, we receive your name and email from Facebook in accordance with Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In: used for optional sign-in. When you log in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: used to display advertisements on ad-supported plans. AdMob may use device identifiers to show contextual ads. The app does not request permission to track you across other apps (no IDFA). See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: used to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat processes purchase receipts and subscription status on our behalf. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: used solely for crash reporting and app stability diagnostics. See Firebase's Privacy Policy.
👶

4. Children's Privacy

Maximize Magic does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. The app is designed to be family-friendly and safe. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

🛡️

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any information processed by the app. Since we do not store personal data on our own servers, the risk of data exposure is minimized by design.

⚖️

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your data, including access, correction, or deletion. Since we collect minimal identifying information, most requests can be fulfilled simply by uninstalling the app and revoking access from your Facebook or Google account settings.

For any data-related requests, please contact us using the information below.

🗑️

7. Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated local data at any time directly within the app:

  • Open the app → Settings → Delete Account. This signs you out of Facebook and Google, and permanently deletes all preferences, saved parks, plans, and session data stored on your device. The app will close automatically after deletion.
  • To revoke the app's access to your Facebook account, go to Facebook → Settings → Apps and Websites and remove Maximize Magic.
  • To revoke the app's access to your Google account, go to Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access and remove Maximize Magic.

Since we do not store personal data on our own servers, completing the in-app deletion constitutes full account deletion. No further action is required.

🔄

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
