Remove Maximize Magic from your Facebook account

Follow these steps to remove all activity and data linked to Maximize Magic from your Facebook account.

1

Go to your Facebook account and click on Settings & Privacy, then click Settings.

2

In the left menu, go to Apps and Websites. You'll see all apps and websites connected to your Facebook account.

3

Find Maximize Magic in the list and check the box next to it.

4

Click Remove. Select your preferences in the dialog and confirm by clicking Remove again.

5

✅ Done! Maximize Magic has been removed from your Facebook activity and all associated data has been deleted.
